# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in discussions.
- Focus on technical facts and reproducible evidence.
- Accept and give feedback in good faith.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces including issues, PRs, discussions, and review comments.
